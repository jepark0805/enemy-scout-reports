Coverage, definitions & reproducibility
  • Only the report date’s filenames with raw EVTC map ID 968 are eligible. Parsed encounter start dates and map IDs are checked again.
  • Full-fight phase only; subphases are not added again. The source is Elite Insights detailed WvW JSON.
  • Only enemyPlayer=true, non-fake targets are enemies. Friendly NPCs and non-squad friendlies are excluded from squad context.
  • Enemy outgoing damage, CC, received strips, downs and deaths reflect what this recording captured. Enemy outgoing damage can include recipients outside your squad. Damage to enemies from your squad is summed separately from squad members’ per-target damage.
  • Team IDs are raw identifiers. Edge of the Mists teams are not relabeled using WvW world colors, and a team ID is not a guild identity.
  • Enemy healing, cleanse performance and reliable boon uptime are unavailable. Weapon and proc-based gear/trait hypotheses are shown with supporting evidence; complete builds and armor stats remain unknown. Missing enemy boons do not mean absent boons.
  • Enemy appearances are not unique people across the session. Neither damage rankings nor observed composition establish causality or a guaranteed counter.
  • The default main team is the team with the most enemy appearances. Select All observed teams to include incidental opponents.
  • Counter experiments are transparent heuristics based on the selection. They are not claims about the current balance patch.
  • Original logs are unchanged. This HTML is self-contained and works offline. Export contains selected summary evidence, not raw combat logs.
